## State contract

Boolean variables: `elian_alive`, `notebook`, `bird`, `bond`, `retained_clues`, `public_warning`, `pip_safe`, `pip_injured`, `seized`, `hearing`, `invited`, `crown_connection`. Integer: `witnesses`. Inventory and learned knowledge use the existing engine's collections. The witness count records independently gained support, not an on-screen population or morality meter. Three groups are needed to call, hold and verify divided shares. Three is stated in the release ledger before the choice appears.

`bond` records recognition retained at the first tithe. It never silently becomes true again after a friendly scene. `pip_injured` never resets at a branch join. Death is irreversible in every ending. A written record may reconstruct facts but is never treated as restoring felt recognition.

`retained_clues` is an editorial/state distinction; the discovery journal remains a factual record in both memory routes. It does not pretend the player forgot words already read. Conditional prose describes Reed's loss of lived context separately.

## Endings

| ID | Title | Eligibility | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `return` | Four Steps | At least three independent groups of support | Living owners recover memories; Reed carries unclaimed fragments; road opens; governance and grief remain work. |
| `vessel` | The Visitor Book | Recovered conscious connection through the garden memory | Others recover; Reed survives with skills but loses her autobiographical history. Her future relationships require fresh consent. |
| `break` | The Names They Chose | Always available | Road opens; pledged relationship-memory and attached recognition are permanently lost. Institutions must protect strangers from compelled family roles. |
| `renew` | The Maintenance Book | Always available | Reed sleeps; tithe doubles while the root repairs. The release terms survive in public records, without promising a future rescue. |

No ending is designated canon for the shared world. There are no unpublished failure nodes, timed penalties or unwritten epilogues.

## Reader behaviour

The existing NIGHT player loads `/stories/?story=crown-of-thorns`. The story's new `fixedIdentity` flag suppresses personal name entry; discovering her identity remains part of the narrative. Conditional identity labels show only what the run has discovered. The Last Call retains its editable name and attribute allocation.

Saves are local to `night-story:crown-of-thorns:v3`; content revision is 1. The other story's key is unchanged. Previous is read-only within the existing decision boundary. Chapter replay creates a separate playthrough. No cloud synchronisation, global achievement or account XP is invented.
